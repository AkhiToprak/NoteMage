The light-dependent reactions

Inside the thylakoid membrane, chlorophyll absorbs photons and pushes excited electrons down the electron transport chain — splitting water and releasing oxygen along the way.

format_list_numberedHow energy is captured
1
Absorb light
Chlorophyll in Photosystem II excites electrons.
2
Split water
Water donates electrons; O₂ is released.
3
Bank the energy
The chain builds ATP and NADPH for the Calvin cycle.

Those carriers don’t power the cell directly — the Calvin cycle spends the ATP and NADPH to build sugar from CO₂.
