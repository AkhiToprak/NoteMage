## Analytics

Notemage uses **PostHog** to understand how the product is used so we can improve it. Analytics are **opt-in**: the first time you visit, we ask for your consent, and **no analytics data is collected unless you allow it**. You can decline, and you can change your decision later by contacting us.

When you consent, PostHog records which pages you view and how you interact with features. If you are signed in, these events are linked to an identifier for your account. This data is processed by PostHog on servers in the **European Union**. Session recording is not used.

PostHog acts as a data processor on our behalf.
