# Refund Policy

This Refund Policy explains when and how you can get a refund for a paid **Notemage** subscription. It applies to Pro subscriptions purchased through our checkout, which is operated by our payment provider, Paddle.

Notemage is operated by Toprak Demirel, an individual based in Switzerland (the "Provider", "we", "us"). If you have any questions, contact us at notemage.app@gmail.com or through our [contact page](/contact).

## 14-day money-back guarantee

We want you to be happy with Notemage Pro. If you are not satisfied, you can request a **full refund within 14 days of your first Pro payment** — no questions asked.

To qualify:

- The request must reach us within **14 days** of the initial purchase.
- The guarantee covers your **first** Pro subscription payment (your initial upgrade), not later renewals (see *Renewals* below).
- We may decline repeated refund requests from the same person where there are clear signs of abuse — for example, repeatedly subscribing and refunding to keep using paid features for free.

If your request meets these conditions, we refund the full amount you paid for that subscription period.

## How payments and refunds are handled

Our payments are processed by **Paddle** (Paddle.com Market Ltd.), which acts as the **Merchant of Record** for purchases of Notemage Pro. This means Paddle is the seller of record for the transaction, handles billing and applicable taxes (such as VAT), and processes refunds on our behalf.

When a refund is approved:

- It is issued to the **original payment method** used for the purchase.
- Paddle processes the refund; the amount typically appears within **5–10 business days**, depending on your bank or card issuer.
- Any taxes collected at purchase are refunded together with the payment.

You may also see Paddle named on your bank or card statement and on your purchase receipt.

## Your right of withdrawal (EU/EEA consumers)

If you are a consumer in the EU or EEA, you have a statutory right to withdraw from a distance contract within **14 days** without giving a reason. Our 14-day money-back guarantee above is designed to meet or exceed this right.

Because Notemage is a digital service that starts immediately, you may be asked at checkout to confirm that you want access right away and that you acknowledge your withdrawal right ends once the service has been fully provided. Even so, our voluntary 14-day guarantee continues to apply as described above.

## Cancelling your subscription

You can cancel your Pro subscription at any time from your account settings or through Paddle's customer portal.

- When you cancel, you keep Pro access until the **end of the period you have already paid for**.
- Cancelling stops future renewals — it does not, by itself, trigger a refund of the current period, except within the 14-day window described above.

## Renewals

Pro subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each billing period until you cancel.

- To avoid being charged for a new period, cancel **before** the renewal date.
- If you are charged for a renewal you did not intend — for example, you meant to cancel but forgot — contact us **within 14 days** of the renewal charge. We will review the request and, where the renewed period has not been meaningfully used, refund it. We think that is the fair thing to do.

## Free plan

The Notemage Free plan has no charges, so there is nothing to refund. You can use, downgrade to, or stop using the Free plan at any time.

## How to request a refund

Email **notemage.app@gmail.com** or use our [contact page](/contact). To help us process your request quickly, please include:

- the email address on your Notemage account, and
- the approximate date of the payment (and an order or receipt reference from Paddle, if you have it).

We aim to respond within a few business days.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the date below and, where appropriate, let you know. The version in force at the time of your purchase applies to that purchase.
