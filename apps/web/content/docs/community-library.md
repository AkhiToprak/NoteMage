The **community library** is where learners share the paths they've built. Found a great path for organic chemistry or French verbs? Add it to your own Learn hub and start studying — no generation required.

## Browsing

Open **/learn/community**. You can:

- **Filter** by subject and language.
- **Filter** by length — how many checkpoints a path has.
- **Sort** by most popular, most recent, or highest rated.

Each path shows how many people have added it, how many have viewed it, and its rating, so you can tell the polished ones from the rough drafts at a glance.

## Adding a path

Open any community path and add it to your library. You get your own copy under **/learn/paths** — your progress, stars, and retakes are yours and never touch the original.

Because it's a copy, **adding a community path doesn't use any AI path allowance**. It works on every plan, Free included — it's how Free learners get full paths without generating their own.

## Publishing your own

Built something worth sharing? Publish it from the path's own page.

Before a path goes live it's **reviewed** — an automated check first, with a human pass whenever something needs a closer look — so the library stays clear of spam and junk. Once it's approved, it shows up for everyone to find. You can unpublish at any time.
