A **learning path** turns a pile of notes into a guided course. Instead of a flat list of flashcards and quizzes, a path lays your material out as a sequence of short lessons, drills, and graded checkpoints you work through in order — the way a language app walks you from "hello" to a real conversation.

Paths live in the **Learn hub**, separate from your notebooks. A notebook is where you *write* and *import*; the Learn hub is where you *study*.

## The Learn hub

**/learn** is the home for everything you study. It pulls together:

- **Paths** — your guided courses (**/learn/paths**).
- **Flashcards** and **Quizzes** — every set you've made, in one place instead of buried inside individual notebooks.
- **Chats** — your study conversations with Mage Chat.
- **Community** — paths other learners have published (**/learn/community**). See [Community library](/docs/community-library).

The hub opens on a **Continue** row, so you can jump straight back into whatever path you were working through.

## Creating a path

From **/learn/paths**, hit **New path**. A path is generated from material you already have, so you'll:

1. **Pick a notebook** to draw from.
2. **Select the source material** — the pages, documents, flashcard sets, and quizzes you want the path built around. Pick at least one.
3. *(Optional)* **Describe your goal** — e.g. "prep for the midterm, focus on the second half." This steers what the AI emphasises.
4. **Choose a language** for the generated content.
5. *(Pro)* Toggle **Ultra** for a slower, deeper build with richer theory and more detail.

A path is always built from real material in a notebook — it can't be conjured from a bare topic with nothing behind it. Import your slides or notes first (see [Documents and imports](/docs/documents-and-imports)), then build the path on top.

## How generation works

Generation runs in two stages, so you're not stuck staring at a spinner:

1. **The outline appears first.** The AI reads your material, works out the subjects, and lays down the phases and checkpoints — titles and objectives — within a few seconds.
2. **The lessons fill in behind it.** Each checkpoint's theory, flashcards, and quiz are written in the background. A progress indicator shows how far along it is, and checkpoints become playable as they finish.

You can leave the page — generation keeps running.

## What's inside a path

Every path is **phases → checkpoints → activities**.

- A **phase** is a unit or chapter — "Section 1: The Cell," "Section 2: Metabolism."
- A **checkpoint** is one stop on the path. Each has a kind:

| Checkpoint | What it's for | What's inside |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Learning** | Teach one new idea | A short theory lesson + flashcards |
| **Review** | Consolidate earlier checkpoints | Flashcards + a quiz |
| **Assessment** | Gate the end of a phase | A quiz only — you must pass to move on |
| **Final exam** | Cover the whole path | One comprehensive quiz |

- **Activities** are the things you actually do at a checkpoint: read a **theory** lesson (with key points, examples, and diagrams), drill **flashcards**, or take a **quiz**.

## Playing through

Checkpoints unlock in order — finish one to open the next. **Assessment** checkpoints are gates: you have to score at least **70%** to unlock the next phase. Everything before an assessment stays open, so you can keep practising while you work toward the gate.

Assessments are scored in **stars**:

- **★☆☆** — 70–84%
- **★★☆** — 85–99%
- **★★★** — 100%
- Below 70% is a miss — the phase stays locked, but you can **retake** the quiz as many times as you need. Your best result stands.

The **final exam** works the same way: pass it and you've finished the path.

## Limits

AI path generation is a **Pro** feature.

- **Free** — browse and clone paths from the [Community library](/docs/community-library); generating your own with AI is Pro-only.
- **Pro** — unlimited standard paths, plus up to **3 Ultra paths per month**.

See [Plans and limits](/docs/plans-and-limits).
